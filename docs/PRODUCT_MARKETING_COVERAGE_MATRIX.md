## Release constraints

- Marketing prices are public display values only. Stripe products, prices, coupons, subscription state, tax calculation, payment processing and tenant entitlements are not changed here.
- Subscription amounts are USD. Tenant selling currency for estimates, invoices and payment records is a separate CAD/USD Business Finance setting.
- Do not market internal Sales CRM / Email SDR, unsupported general transactional SMS, automatic tax filing/remittance, general-ledger accounting, continuous employee tracking, medical-record certification, or unverified integrations.
- The exact trial and payment-information behavior remains governed by the checkout flow. Public copy uses “eligible workspace” and directs visitors to checkout terms rather than making a card or cancellation promise.
